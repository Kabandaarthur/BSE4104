## Retake eligibility

A student is allowed to retake a failed course in the semester immediately
after the one in which the course was failed. A retake is a re-sit of the same
course; the student attends the course again and re-sits its final
examination.

## Repeating a course

If a student fails the same course more than once, or fails a required core
course, the department may require the student to repeat the course rather
than retake it. A repeated course counts as a fresh enrolment and must be paid
for in full.

## Retake fees

A retake of a failed course attracts a retake fee determined by the
University Bursar's office and published before the retake window opens. Fee
payment must be confirmed before the student is permitted to sit the retake
examination.

## How to apply for a retake

To apply for a retake, a student submits a retake application form to the
academic department office during the retake application window. The window
closes two weeks before the retake examination sitting. Applications received
after the window closes are not accepted.

## Where to get help with retakes

Questions about retake eligibility, fees or application windows should be sent
to the academic department office. Questions about the retake fee amount
should be sent to the University Bursar's office.
