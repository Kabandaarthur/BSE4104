## Registration window

The registration window for the normal academic year opens on the first
Monday of the semester and closes at the end of the second week of the
semester. Students are expected to complete registration within this window for
each course they are enrolled on.

## Late registration

Students who fail to register within the normal window may still register for
up to two weeks after the closing date. Late registration attracts an
administrative late fee. To register late, a student must submit a late
registration form through their academic department office together with a
written explanation of why they missed the normal window.

## What you need to register

To complete registration a student needs: a valid student registration number,
a scanned identity photo, confirmation of fee payment from the Bursar's office,
and the course list for the semester. Any course the student has not paid for
is not registered.

## Registration documents

All registration documents are issued electronically once registration is
confirmed. Students who need a printed copy can request one from the College
Registrar's office; printed copies take up to three working days to be ready.

## Where to get help with registration

Questions about the registration window or the late registration process
should be directed to the academic department office or the College
Registrar. Fee-related questions should be directed to the University
Bursar's office.
