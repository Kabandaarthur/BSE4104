## Course add window

New courses can be added to a student's semester load during the first two
weeks of the semester. During this window, additions are approved by the
academic department office and do not require a fee. Courses added after the
second week require a late-add application.

## Course drop window

A course can be dropped without academic penalty during the first four weeks
of the semester. A drop after the fourth week is recorded on the student's
academic record as a withdrawal and may still require the course fee.

## How to add or drop a course

Course additions and drops are submitted using the course add/drop form
provided by the academic department office. The form must be signed by the
course lecturer and the department head, then returned to the department
office for processing.

## Minimum and maximum load

A student is expected to carry a minimum of three and a maximum of seven
courses per semester. A student who drops below the minimum load must obtain
special approval from the department head to continue their study load.

## Where to get help with course changes

Questions about add/drop windows and forms should be directed to the academic
department office or the College Registrar's office.
