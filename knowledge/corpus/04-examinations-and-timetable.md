## The examination timetable

The final examination timetable is published at least two weeks before the
first examination sitting. The timetable shows the date, time and venue for
every course examination. Students are expected to confirm their own
examination timetable and to arrive at the venue at least thirty minutes
before the scheduled start time.

## Clashing or missing examination details

If a student finds that two examinations are scheduled at the same time, or
that a course examination is missing from the timetable, they should report
the problem to the Examinations Office immediately. Examinations Office staff
make the final decision on how the clash is resolved.

## Examination admission

A student is admitted to an examination venue only with a valid student
identity card and the examination timetable entry for that examination.
Students are not admitted to an examination without having completed
registration for the course.

## Examination conduct

During an examination, students may not bring into the venue any unauthorised
material, phone, or electronic device. Any suspected breach of examination
conduct is referred to the disciplinary process of the University and is
decided by the relevant University committee, not by the Student-Support Case
Agent.

## Where to get help with examinations

Questions about the timetable, venues or examination clashes should be
directed to the Examinations Office. Questions about results or grading
should be directed to the course lecturer or the College Registrar.
