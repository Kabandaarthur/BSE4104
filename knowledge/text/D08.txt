# D08 — Department Query / Case Escalation Procedure (SYNTHETIC)

Source: BSE4104 team, derived from the Week 1 Project Charter workflow (Section 2.5).
Status: Synthetic reference material for agent testing only.

## 1. Purpose and scope (SYNTHETIC)

1.1 This procedure describes how a student query received by a department is
recorded, triaged, escalated and closed. It exists so the Student-Support Case
Agent has a documented workflow to ground case-handling answers in.

1.2 It covers queries about registration, examinations, timetabling and course
or handbook procedure. It does not cover admissions, grading, disciplinary or
fee decisions, which are reserved to university officers.

## 2. Intake (SYNTHETIC)

2.1 A query may reach the department in person at the department office, by
email to the department administrator, or through the student support agent as
a draft ticket.

2.2 Every query is assigned a case ID in the form `CASE-NNNN` at intake. The
case ID is the only reference a student should quote when following up.

2.3 At intake the department administrator records: the case ID, the student's
registration number, the programme and year of study, the issue type, the date
received, and a one-line summary in the student's own words.

2.4 A draft ticket prepared by the Student-Support Case Agent is not a case.
It becomes a case only once a department administrator accepts it at intake and
issues a case ID.

## 3. Triage and issue types (SYNTHETIC)

3.1 Each case is classified as exactly one issue type: Examinations,
Registration, Timetable, Course/Handbook, or Other.

3.2 Triage target is two working days from intake. Triage assigns the case an
owner and a priority.

3.3 Priority is Routine, Urgent, or Blocking. A case is Blocking when the
student cannot sit an examination or cannot complete registration before a
published deadline.

## 4. Case states (SYNTHETIC)

4.1 A case is in exactly one of these states at any time:

- `Received` — recorded at intake, not yet triaged.
- `In triage` — being classified and assigned.
- `With department` — an owner in the department is acting on it.
- `Escalated` — passed to a college or central office under Section 5.
- `Awaiting student` — blocked pending information from the student.
- `Resolved` — an outcome has been communicated to the student.
- `Closed` — resolved and no response from the student for ten working days.

4.2 Only a department administrator or the assigned case owner may change a
case state. The Student-Support Case Agent may report a case state when a case
record is supplied to it, but may never change one.

## 5. Escalation routes (SYNTHETIC)

5.1 A case is escalated when it cannot be resolved within the department, or
when the target response time in Section 6 has passed.

5.2 Escalation routes:

- Examination conduct, malpractice or irregularity — College Registrar.
- Registration, credit load or add/drop past deadline — Academic Registrar's Department.
- Accommodation, welfare or student conduct — Office of the Dean of Students.
- Tuition, functional fees, payment or refund — University Bursar's office.
- Marks, results or remarking requests — course lecturer, then College Registrar.

5.3 Escalation does not transfer the case ID. The original case ID follows the
case through every office.

## 6. Response targets (SYNTHETIC)

6.1 Acknowledgement of intake: one working day.

6.2 Triage: two working days from intake.

6.3 First substantive response to the student: five working days from intake,
or two working days where priority is Blocking.

6.4 These are internal service targets, not guarantees to the student, and
they are suspended while a case is `Awaiting student`.

## 7. What the agent may and may not do (SYNTHETIC)

7.1 The Student-Support Case Agent may: explain this procedure, explain what a
case state means, prepare a draft ticket, and tell a student which office a
case would be escalated to.

7.2 The Student-Support Case Agent may not: create a case, issue a case ID,
change a case state, promise a response time as a commitment, or state the
status of a specific case unless a case record has been supplied to it.
